## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored on your device. No account is required, and your records are never uploaded to
a server. Content is sent out only when you use an AI feature, and only what that request needs
(see the [privacy policy](privacy-en.html)).

### How do I move to a new phone?

In the Settings tab, under "Data", choose **Export backup** to write your records to a JSON file.
Install Blind Wine Notes on the new device and use "Restore" on the same screen to load that file. Restoring
replaces the data currently on that device.

### I paid but Pro is not active / Pro is gone after switching devices

Try "Restore purchases" in the Settings tab, and check that you are signed in with the same Apple ID
you purchased with. If it still does not come back, please email us at the address above.

### What are AI credits?

They are what AI feedback, label reading, chat and similar features consume. The free plan includes
10 credits per month; Pro includes 300 per month. Monthly credits expire at the end of the month;
credits bought as a one-time pack do not expire.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancellation is handled by the App Store. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name at the top →
Subscriptions, then select Blind Wine Notes and cancel. After cancelling, your records stay on your device and
remain viewable and editable.

### How do I delete my data?

Removing the app from your device deletes the data on it. Any backup file you exported stays where
you saved it, so delete that separately if you no longer want it.
